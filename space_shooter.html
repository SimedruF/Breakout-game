🚀 SPACE SHOOTER 🚀

Controls

Move: Arrows / WASD
Shoot: SPACE
Pause: P
Mute: M

Features

  • Descending enemy ships
  • Rotating asteroids
  • ⚡ Level 3: Rapid Fire becomes PERMANENT
  • ⚡ Level 5: Dual Guns becomes PERMANENT
  • Power-up Shield (S) - temporary shield
  • Power-up UPGRADE (U) - bigger golden ship
  • Particle systems for explosions
  • Increasing difficulty per level
  • Locally saved high score
