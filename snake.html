🐍 SNAKE

The Classic Arcade Snake Game

Controls

Move: ← → ↑ ↓ Arrows
Alt Move: W A S D
Pause: P
Mute: M
Start: SPACE
Restart: SPACE

Features

  • Classic snake gameplay with wrap-around edges
  • Progressive speed increase as you eat more
  • High score saved locally
  • Sound effects
  • Simple, clean graphics with grid
