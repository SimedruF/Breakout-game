🐸 FROGGER

Cross the Road and River to Reach Safety!

Controls

Move: ← → ↑ ↓ Arrows
Alt Move: W A S D
Pause: P
Mute: M
Start: SPACE
Restart: SPACE

Features

  • Classic Frogger gameplay — cross roads and rivers
  • 5 road lanes with cars, trucks, and buses
  • 5 river lanes with logs and turtles to ride
  • 5 goal slots to fill per level
  • Timer countdown adds pressure
  • Progressive difficulty — traffic speeds up each level
  • 3 lives, high score saved locally
  • Modern visuals with glow effects and animations
